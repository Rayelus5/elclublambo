# F4d — Sorteos verificables — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A treasurer creates a raffle with a USDC or WETH prize, a close date and a mode (equal, or weighted by participaciones where SuperAdmin has switched that on for the network). Members join it. When it closes, the winner comes from a commit-reveal seed plus the hash of the first Linea block after the close, and anyone can recompute the result in their browser. The prize is a fund liability from creation until it is paid or cancelled, and the payout is verified on chain like a withdrawal. A prize unpaid after 14 days is marked "impagado".

**Architecture:**
- **SQL owns the lifecycle** in `raffles` / `raffle_entries`, through SECURITY DEFINER RPCs.
  - Member-side calls (enter, leave, create, cancel, switch) run with the member's JWT.
  - Closing, drawing and recording payouts need chain reads, so they run as service role from the cron or a server action after viem has read the chain.
- **The secret seed never leaves the database before the draw.** It is generated in SQL (`extensions.gen_random_bytes(32)`) and stored in `private.raffle_seeds`, a schema that no API role can use. Only `keccak256(seed)` is published. The seed is copied into `raffles.seed_reveal` and deleted at the draw.
- **The draw is computed in SQL and mirrored in TS.** `_keccak256` (plpgsql) and `_raffle_pick` on one side, `raffle-draw.ts` (viem) on the other, pinned by one shared JSON fixture. The SQL result is what counts, and the TS mirror powers the public verifier.
- **Prizes are liabilities.** `_dao_liabilities_usd6` counts them, valuing WETH at the caller's ETH price. Creating a raffle lowers the cached NAV by the prize. Cancelling or voiding restores it. A recorded payout leaves the cache alone, the same F4c pattern as approve, reject and payout.
- **Payouts reuse the F4c machinery:**
  - the scan links a matching outflow and raises no false alert;
  - TS verifies it with `atLeast`, now in USDC or WETH;
  - the payout is recorded at confirmations, which ends the liability from its block on;
  - the raffle is marked `paid` at finality;
  - not-found marks and unlinking work as for withdrawals.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16 App Router (cacheComponents, server actions), Supabase Postgres (definer RPCs with `search_path=''`, pgTAP), viem, Vitest + RTL, Playwright on the local Hardhat chain 59141 (mock USDC, WETH and price feed).

## Global Constraints
- **Money:**
  - Amounts are `numeric(78,0)`: USDC with 6 decimals, WETH in wei (18).
  - Weights and shares are `numeric(78,0)`.
  - TS uses `bigint` only, never floats.
  - RPCs return large numbers as text.
  - Market values are labelled `USD`, and prizes keep their token, `USDC` or `WETH` (R7).
- **Prize:**
  - `prize_token in ('USDC','WETH')`.
  - Minimum: `1_000000` for USDC, `10^15` wei for WETH (`prize_too_small`).
  - `prize_value_usd6 = USDC ? amount : floor(amount · eth_price_e8 / 10^20)`, where the price is that of the chain's latest `dao_nav_snapshots`. It must be `≤ dao_state.value_usdc` (`prize_too_large`).
- **Liabilities (my decision 1).** `_dao_liabilities_usd6(p_chain, p_at_block, p_eth_price_e8 default null)` = the latest definition (`20261019300005`), plus for each raffle of the chain with `status in ('open','closed','drawn','paid')`:
  - with `p_at_block` null: the raffles that are not `paid` and have `payout_tx_hash is null`;
  - with a block: `payout_block is null or payout_block > p_at_block`.

  Each one counts at its value, USDC as is and WETH as `floor(amount · price / 10^20)`. The price is `coalesce(p_eth_price_e8, latest snapshot price of the chain, 0)`.
- **Cached NAV (`dao_state.value_usdc`), in the same transaction:**
  - create: `value_usdc := greatest(value_usdc − prize_value_usd6, 0)`;
  - cancel or void: `value_usdc := value_usdc + prize_value_usd6`;
  - payout recording and finality: unchanged.
- **Lock order:** raffle row `for update` → `chain_config for share` (when read) → `dao_state for update` → `dao_positions`.
  - The exception is the scan, which takes `chain_config` first and then the matched raffle row `for update of r`, as for withdrawals (header of `20261019300004`).
- **Statuses:** `open | closed | drawn | paid | cancelled | void`. These flags are derived, never stored:
  - `unpaid` = `status = 'drawn' and payout_tx_hash is null and drawn_at < now() − 14 days`;
  - `paid_late` = `status = 'paid' and payout_block_time > drawn_at + 14 days`.
- **Member-facing labels** (exact, from `raffleStatusLabel`):
  - `Abierto`
  - `Cerrado · esperando el bloque del sorteo`
  - `Resuelto · pendiente de pago`
  - `Pago detectado · esperando finalidad`
  - `Impagado · más de 14 días sin pagar`
  - `Pagado`
  - `Pagado con retraso`
  - `Cancelado`
  - `Sin ganador`
- **Draw (my decision 9):**
  - `commit = keccak256(seed)`, with the seed as 32 bytes.
  - `random = uint256(keccak256(seed ‖ draw_block_hash))`: 64 bytes, read big-endian.
  - `r = random mod total_weight`.
  - Walk the entries by `position` ascending, summing weights. The winner is the first position whose running sum is `> r`, so entries with weight 0 never win. `total_weight = 0` means no winner (`void`).
  - All values are lowercase `0x` hex.
- **Draw block:**
  - It is the block with `timestamp > closes_at` whose parent has `timestamp ≤ closes_at`.
  - It is used only once it is `≤` the `finalized` block. The seed is revealed only then.
- **Weights at close (my decision 10):**
  - `position` is assigned in `(entered_at, member_id)` order.
  - Equal: 1 for an active member, else 0.
  - Weighted: `greatest(dao_positions.shares − locked_shares, 0)` on the raffle's chain for an active member, else 0.
  - The club fund has no member row, so it never enters.
  - The weights never change after close.
- **Privacy (my decision 10):**
  - In entry lists, the viewer sees themself with their own label and `isMe`.
  - A member with `dao_ranking_visible = false` shows as `Anónimo #n`. `n` is their 1-based rank among the hidden entries in list order.
  - On cards a hidden winner is `Anónimo`, though they still get their notice.
  - A payout's `tx_hash` goes only to treasurers and the winner.
  - Treasurers see the winner's real label and wallet, because they need them to pay.
- **Switch (user decision 10):**
  - `chain_config.weighted_raffles_enabled boolean not null default false`. The migration sets it to true for 59141.
  - Only bit 512 changes it.
  - It gates **creating** weighted raffles only.
  - The legacy `app_config.weighted_raffles_enabled` key stays untouched and unused (test 010 pins it).
- **Payout match:**
  - one `Transfer` log of `prize_token` from the chain's Safe to `winner_address`;
  - `value ≥ prize_amount`;
  - receipt `success`, the official token contract (WETH from `addressesFor(chain).weth`), `TIP_CONFIRMATIONS` confirmations;
  - block time `≥ drawn_at − 120 s`, with no upper bound (a late payment is still a payment);
  - `(chain_id, tx_hash, log_index)` unused by any other raffle payout or withdrawal payout, and never a `dao_ledger` tx;
  - the raffle is `drawn` with no payout recorded.
  - It becomes `paid` only once `payout_block ≤ finalized` and a re-verification passes.
- **Error codes** (SQL raises them; `src/lib/errors.ts` gets the Spanish copy, exact):
  - `raffle_closed`: `El sorteo ya está cerrado.`
  - `raffle_drawn`: `Un sorteo ya resuelto no se puede cancelar: el premio es del ganador.`
  - `weighted_raffles_disabled`: `Los sorteos ponderados no están activados en esta red.`
  - `prize_too_small`: `El premio mínimo es 1 USDC o 0,001 WETH.`
  - `prize_too_large`: `El premio vale más que el fondo.`
  - `closes_at_invalid`: `El cierre debe estar entre 1 hora y 30 días desde ahora.`
  - `title_required`: `Escribe un título de 1 a 80 caracteres.`
  - `draw_block_invalid`: `Ese bloque no es el primero posterior al cierre.`
  - Reused: `forbidden`, `not_found`, `invalid_input`, `invalid_state`, `chain_invalid`, `nav_not_ready`, `reason_required`, `safe_changed`, `safe_change_blocked`, `payout_pending`, `tx_not_found`, `tx_failed`, `tx_mismatch`, `tx_too_old`, `tx_already_used`, `rpc_unavailable`.
- **Notifications:**
  - New kind `raffle_won`, with data `{raffleId, title, token, amount}`, sent to the winner.
  - `treasury_notice` gains the events `raffle_drawn` and `raffle_unpaid`, with data `{event, raffleId}`, sent to every active holder of bit 256.
- **Generic project guards** (existing tests enforce them):
  - RLS on with no client policies;
  - every function `security definer set search_path = ''`;
  - `revoke ... from public, anon` (and from `authenticated` for service-only functions);
  - re-create a function by copying its **latest** definition (named per task) and changing only what the task says.
- **UI:**
  - Spanish copy, existing tokens, targets ≥ 44 px, no new dependencies;
  - color is never the only signal;
  - no horizontal scroll at 375 px;
  - load `ui-ux-pro-max` before styling (project rule).
- **Next.js 16:** read `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` on dynamic route `params` (a Promise) before writing `/dao/sorteos/[id]` (AGENTS.md).
- **Local DB:** `pnpm db:test`, `pnpm test:e2e` and `tests/integration` write the user's local DB. During tasks run single pgTAP files and `pnpm test:unit`; run the full suites only where a step says so and the controller has cleared it (memory rule).

## Review Focus
1. **Someone tries to learn the seed before the draw:** a treasurer through any RPC, a member through PostgREST, or the service role through the API. Expected: no path returns it. `private` is not usable by `anon`, `authenticated` or `service_role`. Every read shows `seedReveal = null` until `drawn` or `void`. Tests in Task 2 (grants) and Task 3 (reads).
2. **Joining or leaving at or after `closes_at`** while the cron has not yet closed the raffle, and a donation or withdrawal request after the close. Expected: `raffle_closed`. The weights are those of the close step and never change afterwards. Test in Task 3.
3. **A pasted hash for the wrong token** (USDC for a WETH prize), for less than the prize, to another wallet, or reused by a withdrawal payout or a donation. Expected: `tx_mismatch` or `tx_already_used`, and nothing recorded. Tests in Task 4 (SQL) and Task 5 (verification).
4. **The share price around a raffle.** Creation lowers it by exactly `prize_value_usd6 / S_eff`, and cancelling restores it. Recording the payout does not move it. A donation credited while a WETH prize is open values the prize at the credit-time price. Tests in Tasks 2 and 4.
5. **No one eligible:** no entries, a weighted raffle where every entrant has 0 unlocked shares, or every entrant banned before the close. Expected: `void`, the seed is revealed, the liability is released, no notice goes out, and the card says `Sin ganador`. Test in Task 3.

- Produces (SQL, internal, no grants):
  - `_keccak256(p_input bytea) returns bytea`: immutable, strict, Keccak-256 with the original `0x01` padding, not SHA-3's `0x06`.
  - `_uint256_be(p_bytes bytea) returns numeric`: big-endian bytes read as an unsigned integer.
  - `_raffle_pick(p_random numeric, p_weights numeric[]) returns int`: the 1-based winning position, or null when the sum is 0 or the array is empty.

- Produces (TS, `raffle-draw.ts`):
  - `raffleCommit(seed: Hex): Hex` = `keccak256(seed)`

## Plan decisions (for the controller's ledger)
- Plan decision: the seed is generated in SQL with `extensions.gen_random_bytes(32)` and stored in `private.raffle_seeds`. That schema grants nothing to `anon`, `authenticated` or `service_role`, and no RPC returns the seed before the draw. Only definer code can read it, so neither members, treasurers nor a leaked service key can predict the winner.
- Plan decision: keccak256 is implemented in plpgsql (`_keccak256`, `bit(64)` lanes), so SQL computes the commit and the draw itself. pgcrypto has no Keccak (its SHA3 pads differently), and the spec fixes keccak256. One shared JSON fixture pins SQL, TS and viem together.
- Plan decision: the draw block must be finalized before the seed is revealed and the winner drawn. Revealing earlier would let a reorg, or the sequencer, grind the block hash with the seed in view. On mainnet this delays the result by hours, and the UI says so.
- Plan decision: weights are snapshotted by the first cron run after `closes_at`, in a step before settle. Joining and leaving are refused from `closes_at` on. Weights cannot be frozen retroactively without history, and changes in that window cannot be exploited because the seed is still secret.
- Plan decision: `position` is the entry order `(entered_at, member_id)`, fixed at close. It is the public "orden estable" the walk and the verifier use.
- Plan decision: weighted weight = unlocked shares (`shares − locked_shares`) on the raffle's chain, so a pending withdrawal counts as locked (spec my decision 10). Inactive or banned members weigh 0 in both modes. The club fund never enters.
- Plan decision: a raffle with total weight 0 at close becomes `void`. The seed is revealed at once, the prize is released and there is no winner.
- Plan decision: every entrant with `dao_ranking_visible = false` shows as `Anónimo #n`, whether or not they are a donor. Hiding only donors would itself reveal who is one. A hidden winner is `Anónimo` on cards.
- Plan decision: the liabilities gain `p_eth_price_e8 numeric default null`, as do `credit_donation`, `quote_withdrawal` and `approve_withdrawal`. TS service callers pass the valuation's price, and a null falls back to the latest snapshot price. This values a WETH prize "al precio del momento del cálculo" without breaking the 30+ existing pgTAP call sites.
- Plan decision: creating a raffle lowers the cached `value_usdc` by `prize_value_usd6`, the prize valued at the latest snapshot price (the price the cache was computed at). Cancel and void add back exactly that stored amount, and payout recording and finality leave the cache alone. This mirrors F4c's approve, reject and payout pattern, so the share price moves exactly once, by the prize.
- Plan decision: `create_raffle` is a member-JWT RPC (bit 256) with no live Safe read. A raffle fixes a token amount rather than computing one from NAV, so the cached NAV suffices; `prize_too_large` guards against promising more than the fund is worth.
- Plan decision: limits are prize ≥ 1 USDC or ≥ 0,001 WETH, a close 1 h–30 d ahead, and a title of 1–80 characters. The spec is silent; the close range matches the polls' range (spec 12).
- Plan decision: only bit 256 creates and cancels raffles (spec §17 puts it in the treasurer panel). The weighted switch is bit 512 per network on `chain_config` (default off, on for 59141) and gates creation only. Equal raffles are always available.
- Plan decision: cancelling is allowed while `open` or `closed`, with a public reason. After the draw it is refused (`raffle_drawn`), because the prize belongs to the winner and an unpaid prize stays visible as `Impagado`.
- Plan decision: a winner banned after the draw keeps the prize, and the panel flags them. This follows F4c-R2: a ban does not confiscate.
- Plan decision: the destination is the winner's login wallet, snapshotted at the draw in `winner_address`, as F4c snapshots `destination_address`.
- Plan decision: the payout window has no upper bound. "Impagado" is a derived flag (`drawn_at + 14 d`), not a status, and a late payment is still recorded and shown as `Pagado con retraso`, like bounties' `paid_late`. The prize stays a liability until it is paid or cancelled (spec my decision 1).
- Plan decision: payouts are two-phase like withdrawals. The payout is recorded at confirmations, which ends the liability from its block on, and becomes `paid` at finality; a permanent failure reverts it, with the same 30-minute not-found marks. The spec says the payout "se verifica igual que una retirada".
- Plan decision: the scan links a matching USDC or WETH outflow to a drawn raffle (to the winner, at least the prize, in the prize token, after `drawn_at − 120 s`), and withdrawal matches take precedence. A real prize payout never raises a false "sin explicar" alert.
- Plan decision: a prize payout's tx hash is shown only to treasurers and the winner, and the public feed note is `Premio de sorteo`. The explorer would reveal a hidden winner's wallet.
- Plan decision: the notifications are `raffle_won` for the winner (spec §19) plus two `treasury_notice` events, `raffle_drawn` and `raffle_unpaid`, for treasurers. There is no notice to every entrant: the card updates live through `DaoLive`.
- Plan decision: raffles have their own page `/dao/sorteos/[id]` with every input and a client-side verifier, and `/dao` shows the cards. The spec asks that "la página del sorteo muestra todos los datos para recalcularlo a mano".
- Plan decision: a Safe change is blocked (`safe_change_blocked`) while the chain has an `open`, `closed` or `drawn` raffle. A prize owed from one Safe cannot be verified against another (extends F4a-R12).
- Plan decision: the treasurer panel's `Avisos` gain unpaid prizes and token shortfalls, where unpaid USDC prizes plus approved withdrawals exceed the Safe's USDC, or WETH prizes exceed the Safe's WETH. These are the raffle facts a treasurer must act on, alongside F4c's.
