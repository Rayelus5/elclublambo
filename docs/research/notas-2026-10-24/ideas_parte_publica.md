# Public Base product for ElClub.: candidate ideas (research as of 2026-10-23)

Scope: what public, no-login web3 tool a solo developer could launch on Base in late 2026 that fits Base funding (Builder Grant, Base Batches, buildathons) and funnels users into the private, invite-only ElClub. forum. Research budget was about 18 tool calls, so the competitor and legal checks are shallow in places. See the Gaps sections.

## Q1. What did Base fund or highlight in 2026, and what patterns win?

### Takeaway
In 2026 Base moved its priorities to **markets/trading, stablecoin payments, and AI agents (x402)**, away from social apps. The Builder Grant (up to $5k) is selective. It requires a live product and **Builder Codes as the first eligibility step**, and its stated focus areas include "consumer apps focused on transaction growth". Base Batches 004 is now a small cohort of 10 fintech/agent startups at $100k each, which is not realistic for a solo side project. The realistic targets are the Builder Grant, Regional Amplification (LatAm "Home Base") and Quests.

### Cited Findings
- Official 2026 strategy (posted 2026-03-31), three priorities: (1) global markets, meaning equities, commodities, **prediction markets**, perps and spot, with the Base App as the place to trade; (2) payments and stablecoins, meaning account abstraction, **stablecoin gas payments, protocol support for memos, policies and rewards**, and Base App for saving, spending, paying others and borrowing; (3) home for builders, meaning agent-native smart accounts, CLI+MCP, and **x402**. Builder programs listed: Base Batches, builder councils, **ERC-8021 builder codes, analytics dashboards, and growth programs that reward users and volume** — [Base, 2026 mission, vision and strategy](https://paragraph-nextjs.paragraph.xyz/@base/2026-mission-vision-and-strategy)

### Inferences
- The winning pattern for a small grant is a **live product with Builder Codes already integrated and measurable onchain tx growth**, preferably in USDC payments or agents/x402. "Social/community" has to be framed as a **transaction product** (payments, escrow, payouts), not as social.
- Base Batches 004 (10 fintech-founder teams) is out of reach for a side project. The Builder Grant, Quests, Regional Amplification via Home Base (LatAm, Spanish-speaking) and regional Devfolio Batch hackathons (LatAm/Europe) are realistic.
- Spanish/LatAm is an angle: Base runs a South America community ("Home Base") and a LatAm Batch hackathon, so a Spanish-first public tool has a ready distribution channel.

## Q2. What are Builder Codes, how are apps measured and rewarded, and what makes an app "Base-native"?

### Takeaway
Builder Codes are ERC-721 NFT identifiers, registered on base.dev, that apps append as an **ERC-8021 calldata suffix** to every transaction. Offchain indexers attribute the txs to the app. That attribution feeds the base.dev analytics, App Leaderboards, Base App store visibility and future rewards. Concrete reward amounts are not public. "Base-native" in 2026 means Base Account / smart wallet support (ERC-5792 `dataSuffix` capability), Base Pay/USDC, gas sponsorship, and x402 for agents.

### Cited Findings
- A Builder Code is an ERC-721 NFT, a unique code like "abc123". Its onchain metadata holds mainly a **payout address for rewards**, and its offchain metadata holds the app's name and site. You get one by registering the project on the Base Dashboard (Settings → Project Settings → Builder Code) — [docs.base.org builder codes](https://docs.base.org/apps/builder-codes/builder-codes)
- Mechanism: an ERC-8021 suffix is appended to the end of calldata. **Contracts need no change or redeploy.** Cost is about 16 gas per non-zero byte. Offchain indexers attribute later. EOAs and smart wallets are both supported (EOAs via `dataSuffix`; ERC-5792 smart wallets via `DataSuffixCapability`; Privy and Turnkey support it). You can verify on an explorer: the last 16 bytes are the repeated `8021` pattern — [docs.base.org](https://docs.base.org/apps/builder-codes/builder-codes); [Base Account dataSuffix capability](https://docs.base.org/base-account/reference/core/capabilities/dataSuffix)
- Rewards: Base attributes activity automatically, "which **unlocks rewards as the program expands**". Apps with codes "can appear in discovery surfaces such as **App Leaderboards, the Base App store, and ecosystem spotlights**". The Dashboard shows spot trading volume, borrow TVL and lending TVL. User metrics and retention you have to track yourself — [docs.base.org](https://docs.base.org/apps/builder-codes/builder-codes)
- Integrations: Privy dataSuffix recipe — [Privy](https://docs.privy.io/recipes/evm/base-builder-codes); CDP `dataSuffix` on `sendUserOperation` — [Coinbase CDP](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/embedded-wallets/evm-features/builder-codes); `ox/erc8021` library — per [search summary of Base docs](https://docs.base.org/base-chain/builder-codes)
- **Base Pay**: one-click USDC payments on Base, no cards or chargebacks, works with every Base Account; docs state no fees for merchant or user — [Base accept payments](https://docs.base.org/base-account/guides/accept-payments); [Base Pay `pay` reference](https://docs.base.org/base-account/reference/base-pay/pay)
- **x402 traction**: Chainalysis via Fortune (2026-10-14) puts cumulative x402 txs on Base at about 138M by Q3 2026, up from about 1,500 in mid-2025, with **87% of payments under $0.10** — [aiweekly.co](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/chainalysis-138m-base-x402-bot-payments-87-under-10-cents); Crypto Briefing cites about 169M as of July 2026 — [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/base-agentic-payments-20-million-transfers/); TRM estimates about **$52.7M across 198.9M settlements** since May 2025 on Base, Solana and Polygon — [TRM Labs](https://www.trmlabs.com/trm-tech-blog/whos-actually-paying-measuring-ai-agent-payments-onchain); Copper argues much of it is wash or self-payments, and genuine Base settlement is around $29M — [Copper Opening Bell 65](https://copper.co/en-ch/insights/research/opening-bell-issue-65). Sources conflict on the totals.

### Inferences
- Builder Codes reward **number and volume of attributed txs**. A public tool where **every user action is a small USDC tx** (pay, tip, enter, payout, attest) is better aligned than a read-only tool such as a scanner or dashboard, which generates zero txs.
- ElClub. already does tips, bounties and raffles onchain. Adding the ERC-8021 suffix to the existing wagmi/viem tx calls is a small change and makes the private club's txs count too.
- x402 is the strongest "agents" narrative for the grant. A solo dev can credibly put an **x402-paid API** in front of something they already have, for example the token or contract check and the link guardian.

### Gaps
- No public figures on Builder Code reward amounts, schedule, or leaderboard criteria.
- Paymaster or gas-sponsorship terms for 2026 were not checked.

## Q3. Candidate public products (evaluated)

Effort scale assumes reuse of the ElClub. codebase (Next.js, wagmi/viem, Supabase, commit-reveal raffle, tips/bounties, Safe treasury, alpha posts with contract verification and DexScreener, link guardian). **All evaluation is inference** unless it carries a citation. "Tx potential" means how many Builder-Code-attributable txs a typical use generates.

### Takeaway
The strongest fits are (1) **public provably-fair giveaway tool paid out in USDC (free entry)**, (2) **USDC bounty and escrow board in Spanish**, (3) **pay/tip links plus x402-paid "token and link safety check" API**, and (4) **onchain vouches/attestations (EAS)** as the public face of the invite tree. Each reuses existing modules, generates real txs, and has a natural "join the club" hook. Paid-entry raffles are a legal red flag in Spain. Keep entry free.

### Cited Findings (competitors, demand, law)
- Raffle/giveaway competitors on Base: **Raflux** (Chainlink VRF on Base, ERC-1155 tickets; sources are promotional) — [Bitrue](https://www.bitrue.com/blog/what-is-raflux); **OnChainWin** (giveaways and ticket raffles on Base, VRF) — [Alchemy dapps](https://www.alchemy.com/dapps/onchainwin); **From Us To You** (daily USDC prize pool with $1, $0.10 and $0.01 entry; listed as **gambling**) — [DappRadar](https://dappradar.com/dapp/from-us-to-you); **FairRaffle.io** (VRF raffle for NFT projects on Twitter/Discord; status unknown) — [Notion](https://fairraffle.notion.site/FairRaffle-io-0c0440a2f2be427495ba60f5c3e134f6). No Discord/Telegram-integrated USDC-on-Base giveaway tool was found — [search, 2026-10-23](https://www.alchemy.com/dapps/onchainwin)
- Pay-link competitors: Base Pay itself is free — [Base docs](https://docs.base.org/base-account/guides/accept-payments); **PayRequest** USDC-on-Base payment links at a **2% fee** — [PayRequest](https://payrequest.io/free-tools/usdc-payment-link-generator)
- Scanner competitors: GoPlus, Token Sniffer, Honeypot.is, De.Fi scanner, Bubblemaps, Arkham — [Plisio guide](https://plisio.net/ja/defi/tools-to-identify-defi-scam-tokens). Scanners often **disagree** (Honeypot.is and GoPlus reached opposite verdicts on the same token) — [Publish0x case file](https://www.publish0x.com/memecoin-daily-insights/can-two-scanners-disagree-about-one-token-the-quq-case-file-xplerjg). A community Apify wrapper combines Honeypot.is and GoPlus with Base support — [Apify](https://apify.com/gochujang/token-honeypot-detector)
- Attestations: Coinbase Verifications run on Base using EAS (verified account, country, Coinbase One schemas) — [Coinbase CDP docs](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/verifications/introduction/attestations); [Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/coinbase-identity-verification-kyc). A vendor claims more than 20M users verified — [InsumerAPI blog](https://insumermodel.com/blog/compliance-gating-coinbase-verifications.html) (unverified)
- **Spain law on draws**: "combinaciones aleatorias con fines publicitarios" are promotional draws **without any surcharge or extra fee**, where the only consideration is consuming the product or service — [RAE Diccionario panhispánico del español jurídico](https://dpej.rae.es/lema/combinaciones-aleatorias-con-fines-publicitarios). A legal-template source says statewide promotional draws need no enabling title or prior notice but are subject to the **Impuesto sobre Actividades de Juego (art. 48 Ley 13/2011)** — [Economist & Jurist](https://www.economistjurist.es/?p=100274) (third party; verify). A DGT ruling (via summary) says the relevant point is whether the draw is authorized, held or organized in Spain — [abogados.com.ar](https://abogados.com.ar/el-impuesto-a-los-premios-es-aplicable-a-los-sorteos-y-concursos-promocionales/24022). No DGOJ guidance specific to crypto draws was found. US precedent: Coinbase was sued over a Dogecoin sweepstakes with a hidden free-entry path — [Decrypt ES](https://decrypt.co/es/76335/fan-de-dogecoin-demanda-a-coinbase-por-sorteo-falso-y-enganoso)

### Inferences
- **Best combo for the grant pitch:** #3 (pay/tip links plus an x402 paywall) as the transaction engine, #1 (free verifiable giveaways) as the viral loop, #4 (x402 safety API) as the "agents" box, and every tx tagged with the Builder Code. This stays within existing modules and fits the stated "consumer apps focused on transaction growth" and "x402 implementations" categories (per [docs.base.org/get-started/builders](https://docs.base.org/get-started/builders)).
- Prediction markets are Base's headline priority, but real-money prediction markets carry high legal risk for a Spanish solo dev. Avoid them or keep them to points only.
- Scanners and dashboards are crowded (GoPlus, De.Fi, Token Sniffer, Honeypot.is, Snapshot) and generate few txs. Their value is as funnel content, not as a grant pitch, unless monetized via x402.
- Giveaway space on Base is thin and leans toward paid-ticket gambling (From Us To You, Raflux). A **free-entry, organizer-funded, Spanish-language** tool with commit-reveal proofs is differentiated and legally safer.
- Spanish/LatAm gap: no Spanish-first Base tool was found in any of these categories. That is weak evidence, since searches were English-centric. Base's "Home Base" South America community gives a ready channel.

### Gaps
- No demand data (search volume, user counts) was found for Spanish-language crypto tools.
- DGOJ's position on crypto-denominated prizes, and whether a platform that hosts third-party draws becomes the "organizer", was not found. This needs a lawyer or a DGOJ consultation.
- MiCA/CNMV crypto-advertising rules and CASP licensing thresholds for escrow and payment links were not researched here. Escrow by smart contract, without the operator holding keys, is presumed lower risk but is not verified.
- Competitors not verified in this pass: Bountycaster/Dework status in 2026, Gleam/Galxe/Layer3/Guild Base support, Splits, Snapshot onchain modules, Scam Sniffer phishing coverage of Base.
- Gas-sponsorship (Paymaster) costs and limits for a free-entry giveaway at scale were not checked.
